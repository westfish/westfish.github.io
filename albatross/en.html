Subscription

Open-library browsing, search, book selection and local file previews are free. Monthly and annual memberships unlock new book downloads and confirmed DRM-free EPUB, TXT and Markdown imports. Both plans provide the same features. Existing saved books, progress and notes stay usable after membership expires. Built-in books, bilingual reading, vocabulary learning, private web-article reading and backups remain free.

Localized prices and billing periods appear in the app and Apple's purchase confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel through Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancellation preserves access until the paid period ends. Restore purchases in the app's membership page using the original purchasing Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Privacy

Reading progress, vocabulary records, assessment answers, notes, imported books, saved articles, illustrations, translation caches and preferences are stored locally in the app. This version provides no app account, cloud sync, public community or cloud pronunciation scoring. No advertising or third-party behavior analytics SDK is included.

Apple handles payments. The app verifies transactions on-device; it does not receive your card number or upload receipts to our own server. User-initiated visits and downloads contact the chosen websites or Project Gutenberg. These providers may receive IP addresses, browser information, requests and cookies under their own policies. Web login data is managed by WebKit and is excluded from learning backups.

Translation and reading aloud use Apple system capabilities; initial translation may require language-pack downloads. Microphone and speech recognition permission are requested only for the recording or spoken practice you choose. Speech recognition may be processed by Apple depending on language and system capabilities. Reminders request notification permission only when enabled.

You can revoke permissions in Settings, delete individual local records, clear website data, export a learning backup or uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not cancel Apple subscriptions. Website hosting and email providers process user-initiated support requests under their own policies.
