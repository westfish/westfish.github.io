Subscription

Library browsing, book acquisition and DRM-free EPUB, TXT and Markdown imports are free. Membership provides Albatross Translation, sequential pretranslation of up to five following pages, and translation-cache reuse. Monthly and annual plans have the same benefits. After expiry, new member translation requests stop; saved books, translations and learning records remain available. Apple system translation and basic reading stay free. Browse Featured Shelves by editorial reading difficulty, or search the larger catalog in All Books. Personal vocabulary counts are shown only for books whose text is available on your device. Select a book and download it directly into your Shelf for free. Original covers and illustrations are retained when available; downloaded books can be read offline. Source terms and any configured regional restrictions still apply.

Localized prices and billing periods appear in the app and Apple's purchase confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel through Apple Account → Subscriptions. Cancellation preserves access until the paid period ends. Restore purchases in the app's membership page using the original purchasing Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Privacy

Reading progress, vocabulary records, assessment answers, notes, imported books, saved articles, illustrations, translation caches and preferences are stored locally in the app. This version provides no app account, cloud sync, public community or cloud pronunciation scoring. No advertising or third-party behavior analytics SDK is included.

Apple handles payments. The app verifies transactions on-device. Enabling Albatross Translation also sends Apple-signed subscription information to our service for membership verification; card details and passwords are not included. A public book-list request does not need membership credentials or learning records. Storefront-region checks stay on device. User-initiated visits and downloads contact the chosen websites or Project Gutenberg, which may receive network information and cookies under their own policies. Web login data is excluded from learning backups.

Apple system translation and reading aloud use system capabilities; initial translation may require language-pack downloads. If you explicitly enable Albatross Translation, text needed for the current page and up to five following pages is sent to our service and its translation providers. The service retains source tasks and translations for completion, retries and cache reuse, isolated by membership. Results are saved on your phone. Switching back stops new submissions; existing requests may finish. Contact support to request deletion of service translation records. Vocabulary assessments, learning marks and notes are not sent for translation. Microphone and speech recognition permission are requested only for the recording or spoken practice you choose. Speech recognition may be processed by Apple depending on language and system capabilities. Reminders request notification permission only when enabled.

You can revoke permissions in Settings, delete individual local records, clear website data, export a learning backup or uninstall the app. Uninstalling does not cancel Apple subscriptions. Website hosting and email providers process user-initiated support requests under their own policies.
